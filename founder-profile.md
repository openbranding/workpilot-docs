## BuildPilot

One of Abayomi’s major current product directions is **BuildPilot**.

BuildPilot is a governed AI software-development workspace designed to help software teams move from project intent to controlled execution, evidence review, verification, acceptance, and handoff.

BuildPilot is not positioned as a coding model. It is a governance and execution workspace for AI-assisted software delivery.

Its doctrine is built around:

- Truth before automation
- Governance before execution
- Evidence before acceptance
- Human authority over irreversible actions
- Controlled execution instead of blind automation

BuildPilot v1.0 is approaching completion and represents a major part of Abayomi’s current work in governed AI-assisted development.

The product reflects his belief that the next stage of AI software development should not only be about generating code, but about controlling how software work is planned, executed, reviewed, verified, and accepted.
