## Development Approach

Workpilot follows a practical and structured delivery approach.

### 1. Understand the Business Problem

We begin by understanding the real business need, the people involved, the workflow, the current pain points, and the outcome the client wants to achieve.

### 2. Define the System Scope

We convert the business need into a clear development scope covering users, roles, workflows, pages, dashboards, database entities, integrations, reports, and expected outputs.

### 3. Design the Architecture

We plan the structure of the application before development begins. This includes data models, routes, modules, permissions, process flow, and technical boundaries.

### 4. Build in Controlled Phases

We develop systems in controlled phases so that each stage can be tested, reviewed, and improved before moving forward.

### 5. Test and Validate

We prioritize working software, not assumptions. Features are reviewed based on whether they actually function and support the intended business workflow.

### 6. Document and Handover

We support delivery with documentation, project reports, capability statements, user guidance, and future roadmap recommendations.
