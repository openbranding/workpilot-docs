## 1. BuildPilot

**BuildPilot** is a governed AI software-development workspace designed to help software teams move from project intent to controlled execution, evidence review, verification, acceptance, and handoff.

BuildPilot is one of Workpilot’s strongest current product directions and is approaching v1.0 completion.

It is not positioned as a coding model. Instead, BuildPilot is positioned as a governance and execution workspace for AI-assisted software delivery.

### Core Idea

AI can help generate code, but software delivery still needs structure, truth, control, evidence, and human approval.

BuildPilot is designed around that gap.

It helps bring discipline into AI-assisted development by supporting a workflow from:

- Project discovery
- Current-state review
- Human confirmation
- Governance registration
- Intent capture
- Scope definition
- Readiness checks
- Roadmap planning
- Execution contracts
- Bounded development tasks
- Runtime execution
- Evidence generation
- Verification
- Human review
- Acceptance
- Handoff

### Doctrine

BuildPilot is built around the following principles:

- Truth before automation
- Governance before execution
- Evidence before acceptance
- Human authority over irreversible actions
- Controlled execution instead of blind automation

### Why BuildPilot Matters

BuildPilot addresses a growing problem in AI-assisted software development.

Many AI tools focus mainly on generating code. BuildPilot focuses on governing the full software delivery process so that generated work can be scoped, controlled, executed, reviewed, verified, and accepted responsibly.

The product is especially relevant for:

- Software teams using AI tools
- Solo builders working with AI assistants
- Agencies managing multiple client projects
- Businesses that need evidence-based delivery
- Developers who want structured AI-assisted workflows
- Product owners who need control over AI-generated work

### Public Status

BuildPilot v1.0 is approaching completion and has reached an impressive stage of development.

The current public positioning is:

> BuildPilot is a governed AI software-development workspace for controlled, evidence-based, AI-assisted software delivery.

## 2. Chioma

**Chioma** is an AI WhatsApp commerce copilot designed to help merchants handle customer conversations, product questions, order flow, and customer support through WhatsApp.

Many small and medium businesses sell through conversation. Customers ask questions, compare products, request prices, ask for availability, and expect quick responses.

Chioma is designed to support this kind of business activity.

### Core Idea

Chioma helps businesses use AI inside WhatsApp commerce without losing the human and conversational nature of selling.

The assistant is designed to help with:

- Customer questions
- Product explanations
- Buying guidance
- Order conversation support
- FAQ handling
- Product knowledge responses
- Human handoff when needed

### Key Focus Areas

- Merchant onboarding
- WhatsApp commerce workflow
- Product and FAQ knowledge structure
- AI-assisted customer response
- Order conversation support
- Customer service automation
- Business support through WhatsApp

### Target Users

Chioma is especially suitable for:

- Retail merchants
- Pharmacies
- Restaurants
- Service businesses
- Small businesses selling through WhatsApp
- Product-based businesses with repeated customer questions
- Businesses that need better customer response without hiring large support teams

### Public Positioning

> Chioma is an AI WhatsApp commerce copilot for merchants who sell, support, and serve customers through conversation.

## 4. BrainBox

**BrainBox** is a deterministic AI control-plane concept for technical operations, software inspection, diagnostics, validation, and safe system execution.

It is not positioned as a casual chatbot.

BrainBox is about structured technical reasoning, system inspection, and controlled execution support.

### Core Idea

Technical systems require more than conversation. They require inspection, diagnosis, validation, memory, evidence, and safe operational workflows.

BrainBox is a concept for using AI in structured technical environments where correctness, control, and traceability matter.

### Key Focus Areas

- AI-assisted infrastructure thinking
- Runtime pipeline planning
- System inspection concepts
- Diagnostics
- Validation and evidence
- Safe execution architecture
- Technical operations support

### Public Positioning

> BrainBox is a deterministic AI control-plane concept for structured technical operations and safe system execution.

## 5. IntentRail

**IntentRail** is a governed development rail designed to turn project intent into structured, scoped, evidence-based execution.

IntentRail focuses on the early and middle stages of AI-assisted software delivery: capturing what should be built, clarifying scope, preparing execution, collecting evidence, and supporting handoff.

### Core Idea

Many software projects fail because intent is unclear, scope drifts, execution is uncontrolled, and evidence is weak.

IntentRail is designed to bring structure into that process.

### Key Focus Areas

- Intent capture
- Scope definition
- Readiness checks
- Execution control
- Evidence collection
- Review discipline
- Handoff preparation
- AI-assisted development governance

### Relationship With BuildPilot

IntentRail and BuildPilot are closely related in direction.

IntentRail focuses on the structured rail from intent to execution readiness and evidence. BuildPilot expands this into a governed AI software-development workspace with runtime execution, verification, acceptance, and handoff.

### Public Positioning

> IntentRail is a governed development rail for turning project intent into scoped, evidence-based execution.

- Procurement workflow planning
