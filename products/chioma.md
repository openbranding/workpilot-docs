# Chioma

**AI WhatsApp Commerce Copilot**  
Merchant Intelligence | Customer Support | Product Knowledge | WhatsApp Commerce Automation

---

## Overview

**Chioma** is an AI WhatsApp commerce copilot designed to help merchants handle customer conversations, product questions, order support, and customer engagement through WhatsApp.

Chioma is part of the Workpilot product ecosystem and represents Workpilot’s direction in practical AI for merchant operations, WhatsApp commerce, customer support, and business knowledge management.

Many businesses already sell and support customers through WhatsApp. Chioma is designed to help those businesses respond faster, organize their product knowledge, support customer questions, and improve the buying conversation without forcing them to abandon the channel they already use.

---

## Core Idea

Chioma exists because WhatsApp is already a major business channel for many merchants.

Customers ask:

- What do you sell?
- How much is it?
- Is it available?
- Can you deliver?
- How do I pay?
- What do you recommend?
- Can I place an order?
- Can I speak with someone?

For many businesses, answering these questions manually every day becomes stressful, slow, and inconsistent.

Chioma is designed to help merchants turn scattered WhatsApp conversations into a more structured, AI-assisted customer engagement process.

---

## What Chioma Helps With

Chioma is designed to support:

- Customer questions
- Product explanations
- Product search
- Frequently asked questions
- Buying guidance
- Order conversation support
- Business policy responses
- Customer support automation
- Merchant knowledge organization
- Human handoff when needed

The goal is not to remove the business owner or staff. The goal is to help the business respond better, faster, and more consistently.

---

## Product Direction

Chioma is being shaped around the idea of a **merchant intelligence platform**.

This means the system is not only about chatting. It is also about helping merchants organize the knowledge their business depends on.

Important areas include:

- Merchant profile
- Product catalog
- Product categories
- Product descriptions
- Pricing information
- Product availability
- Frequently asked questions
- Delivery information
- Payment instructions
- Business policies
- Customer support rules
- Escalation and handoff instructions

When this information is structured properly, AI can support customer conversations more effectively.
