# MerchantPilot

**International AI Commerce Assistant**  
Merchant Automation | Customer Engagement | Product Knowledge | Practical AI for Commerce

---

## Overview

**MerchantPilot** is the international commerce assistant direction within the Workpilot ecosystem.

It is connected to the broader idea behind Chioma, but positioned for wider international merchant use.

MerchantPilot is designed for businesses that need AI-assisted customer conversations, product support, order guidance, customer engagement, and practical commerce automation.

The product direction is focused on helping merchants organize business knowledge, respond to customers faster, support buying decisions, and improve customer service across digital conversation channels.

---

## Core Idea

Many businesses around the world now sell through conversation.

Customers ask questions before they buy. They want product information, price clarity, availability, delivery details, recommendations, and support.

MerchantPilot is designed to help businesses manage these customer interactions with practical AI support.

The core idea is simple:

> Help merchants respond better, sell smarter, and support customers more consistently with AI-assisted commerce workflows.

---

## Relationship With Chioma

MerchantPilot and Chioma are closely related.

**Chioma** is the AI WhatsApp commerce copilot direction shaped around WhatsApp-first merchant realities.

**MerchantPilot** is the international edition direction for broader merchant automation, customer engagement, and commerce support.
